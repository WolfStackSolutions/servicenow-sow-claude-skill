SOW Skill Demodemo

Install

Drag the button to your bookmarks bar, then click it on any SOW page. Press Ctrl+Shift+B if the bar is hidden. Click again to toggle the menu.

SOW Skill Demo drag to your bookmarks bar
  1. suppress
    Alert Suppressor
    Intercepts NOTIFICATIONS_UPDATED dispatch events and blocks now-alert DOM insertion. Logs every suppressed notification with timestamp. Stops focus stealing and dismiss timers.
  2. copy
    Ticket Copy
    Adds a clipboard icon to every ticket tab (INC, RITM, REQ, CHG, PRB, SCTASK, etc). Shows on hover, copies the ticket number on click. Walks shadow DOM with MutationObservers to catch new tabs.
  3. counter
    Tab Counter
    Live count / max badge in the tab strip. Updates every 2 seconds. Colour shifts from neutral to amber near the limit, red when full. Auto-detects light/dark theme.
  4. limits
    Tab Limit Extender
    Raises the workspace tab cap from 10 to 16 and the screen pool from 3 active / 5 cached to 22 / 26. Patches tabConfig and mainConfig on sn-canvas-tabs and sn-canvas-main. Reasserts every 3 seconds when the framework rewrites them. Also stubs the agentic_processing endpoint that 400s every 6 seconds.
  5. associate
    Quick Associate
    Input field on IMS records that accepts INC, REQ, RITM, or SCTASK numbers. Resolves the full parent chain (SCTASK -> RITM -> REQ) and shows a confirmation dropdown before POSTing to interaction_related_record. Only appears on interaction records, auto-removes on navigation away.
  6. whoami
    Who Am I
    One-shot panel showing your session: name, user_name, email, title, sys_id, and token preview. Queries sys_user via gs.getUserID() with current_user fallback (user_sys_id, not sys_id).
  7. myinc
    My Open Incidents
    FAB + scrollable panel listing your active incidents by assigned_to. Shows number, short description, state, priority, and last update. Click a row to open in a new tab. Refresh button to re-query.
