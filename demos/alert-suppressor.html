SOW Alert Suppressor demo

Install

Drag the button to your bookmarks bar, then click it while logged into Service Operations Workspace. Press Ctrl+Shift+B if the bookmarks bar is hidden.

Alert Suppressor drag to your bookmarks bar
  1. layer 0
    Event interception
    Hooks EventTarget.dispatchEvent to capture NOTIFICATIONS_UPDATED payloads before they reach the UI. Extracts the notification message text into a queue.
  2. layer 1
    DOM insertion block
    Patches Node.prototype.insertBefore and appendChild to intercept now-alert elements. Hides them immediately and removes them on the next microtick. No element means no timer, no dismiss animation, and no focus steal.
  3. ui
    FAB + log panel
    Shield icon FAB with a badge counter. Expands into a scrollable log panel showing every blocked notification with timestamp and message text. Clear log button resets the list.
